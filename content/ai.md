---

Everything on this website is written by a human. 

However, I use artificial intelligence for checking the grammar of my blog posts.

This is the prompt I use:

> grammar check the following blog post. don't change the tone or style or anything that can change the meaning. fix the grammar problems in the text and give me the edited text. preserve the original capitalization. list all your modifications separately. if you have any suggestions for improvements for readability and coherence, you are welcome--give me a list.
