---
title: "AI Use"
---

Everything on this website is written by [me](/about), a human. However, I use
artificial intelligence for some things:

- **Proofreading and copy editing**: English is not my native language and I often do silly grammatical errors and/or forget to add articles where necessary. After writing a post, I usually send it to Claude for grammar checking and also ask them about readability. If they have suggestions I like, I apply them.
- **Developing ideas**: I believe developing an idea is a dialogical process. Sometimes I use Claude for talking about a half-baked idea to see if I can develop it further. I instruct Claude to assume an intellectually adversarial persona, so it challenges the idea until it becomes annoying.
