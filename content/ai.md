title: "AI Use"
---

Everything on this website is written by a human. This will always be the case. It's simply meaningless for me to generate text using AI and publish it. This blog exists for me to express myself. 

However, I use artificial intelligence to check the grammar of my blog posts. As of November 1 2026, you can see each edit AI made to my writing by clicking the "[[./posts/Proofread with AI|Proofread with AI]]" links at the end of posts. I hope this provides more transparency about to what extent AI was used on words I've written.
