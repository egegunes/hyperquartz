I spent most of the weekend in Bremen, Germany. Strolling around the city, I encountered two sculptures that I really liked.

<img src="https://hypersubject.b-cdn.net/images/in-the-crosshairs-of-justice-and-the-end-of-bremen/justice.jpeg" width=600 />

I saw this right after I arrived in the city. Before anything, its scale made me stop. At first I thought it was a futuristic scene. Then I realized that it was concretely bureaucratic.

This was sculpted by Manfred Lohrengel in 1973 on the wall of the district court. Its name is _Im Fadenkreuz der Justiz_ ("In the Crosshairs of Justice"). Before reading the Wikipedia page—which is only available in German—I figured it was depicting some kind of hearing.

<img src="https://hypersubject.b-cdn.net/images/in-the-crosshairs-of-justice-and-the-end-of-bremen/justice-1.png" width=600 />

What's going on at the bottom? The woman at the center is the most prominent. She looks captured and broken. Is she the accused or the victim? Her appearance is so out of joint. A naked body and an almost erotic posture.

<img src="https://hypersubject.b-cdn.net/images/in-the-crosshairs-of-justice-and-the-end-of-bremen/justice-2.png" width=600 />

In the upper right, just past her shoulder, you'll notice an angry man shouting at her. Who is he? The offender? The prosecutor?

With all these questions running through my mind, I encountered another gory body buried in a wall: Bernd Altenstein's _Das Ende_.

<img src="https://hypersubject.b-cdn.net/images/in-the-crosshairs-of-justice-and-the-end-of-bremen/das-ende-2.jpeg" height=600 />

The graffito on the upper corner says "wouldn't have happened with glasses on." At first I thought that was part of the sculpture, but Wikipedia—again, only available in German—says it appeared after the piece was erected there in 1978. It's funny because at first glance the sculpture looks like someone ran into a wall. However, the body looks like it was hurled into a wall. There's movement, as if the man is fighting the wall to get inside. As if something on the other side were pushing him away. This is the end for him, but he's not going down without a fight.
