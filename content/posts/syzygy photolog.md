the biggest news of this week was definitely the exhibition at OtonomArt. after ~50 hours of work, it was so exciting to put _Syzygy_ on display. it was also thrilling to see my name as the artist on the label.

i’m glad i photographed the sculpture after every session so i could see the gradual process. now, looking back, i find it eerie to look at the first photos. i created this thing from mud with my bare hands!
