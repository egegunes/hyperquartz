*Notice that the title say why **I** am a Marxist, not why you should be a Marxist. This is a meditation on my beliefs and ideology, not an attempt to convince you.*

What does being a Marxist mean? On my about page, I claim that I'm one. What does that claim try to communicate to the visitor of this website? More importantly what does an average visitor, in my imagination, understand from that statement?

Let's start with what you should not understand. I **don't** think:
- Marx's prescription for fixing the capitalist society was flawless,
- Stalin did nothing wrong and Soviet Russia was a paradise on earth,
- the source of all humanity's problems is capitalism,
- only driving force in history was class struggle and nothing else.

First and foremost, I'm a Marxist because I agree with Marx's critique of capitalist economy. I believe it's fundamentally wrong to arrange the economy in terms of maximizing profits. I believe the economy should be for satisfying human needs.
