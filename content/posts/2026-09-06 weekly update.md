We finished watching *Archive 81*. It was probably the best ghost story I've ever seen. Even though the ending was a bit awkward, it captivated my attention in all 8 episodes. At first I was thinking this was a hauntological story where the spectre-of-never-was haunting the present but then then haunting materialized and became bidirectional. If you're in search of a mini series, I strongly recommend Archive 81.

---

A friend recommended me Agustina Bazterrica's *Tender is the Flesh* (Cadáver Exquisito). It was a short book so I finished it in two sittings. However short it is, it's not something easily digestible. It might be the most harrowing thing I've ever read. It tells a story where all animals on the world are exterminated after the discovery of a deadly virus and humanity decides to breed human beings as a source of protein. If you want to be disgusted by everything, this is the book.
