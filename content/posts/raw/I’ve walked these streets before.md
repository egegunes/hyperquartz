Last week was so busy that I skipped writing a weekly update here. I figured it'd be better if I write an ad-hoc update[^1].

I'm writing this in a hotel room in Long Island, New York City. It's 5:00 AM. This is my first time experiencing jet lag. I can see why people are not a fan.

This was the first time I've spent more than 4 hours on a plane. But it was much better than I anticipated. I finished Zamyetin's the book We and watched two movies: Steven Spielberg's Disclosure Day (2026) and Zack Snyder's Sucker Punch (2011). It was a good opportunity to watch these, I wouldn't pick these movies to watch if I wasn't stuck on a plane with 10 hours to kill. The first hour of Disclosure Day was so good and captivating, unfortunately after that it became kinda boring. Sucker Punch on the other hand was really fun to watch although heartbreaking. 

Anyway, after we landed and went through the customs to enter USA, we took an Uber to Long Island and checked in to our hotel. We didn't plan to much to do today. We grabbed a quick food from a food truck and then went to Midtown Manhattan.

In the subway to Manhattan, the experience had a bizarre turn. Eveything and everyone around us started to seem familiar. I don't mean familiar in a disenchanted way. This familiarity was the enchanment itself. After visiting the Public Library and started strolling around the 5th Avenue, this feeling was heightened even more: I was feeling like I already knew these places, even lived here before!

We didn't do much except walking around the 5th Avenue, seeing Grand Central and Chrysler Building. At 7:00 PM, we were already in bed. The journey starts today.

[^1]: I'm thinking of dropping the weekly update concept for good. It might be better to write multiple atomic, even small, posts to share life updates. However the best function of weekly updates is they keep you in the game even when you don't feel like playing.
