- Pizza is overrated.

- Statue of Liberty sounded like a tourist trap but much pleasant than I expected.

- People of New York are performatively polite. I don't mean performative in a negative sense but for gestures like saying "sorry" even unwarranted or holding the door for the next person.

- Coffee is good almost everywhere. This surprised me so much. In Istanbul and whenever I travel to Europe I usually need to seek cafes that brew with specialty beans because regular coffee in most places is almost undrinkable. Here on the other hand the regular drip coffee is pretty decent. I hadn't had any urge to commute just for having coffee.
