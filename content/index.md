Hi! I'm ege. Welcome to my corner of cyberspace.  

The following is for you to make sense of who I am. It is an attempt to make myself legible to you. So you can label and categorize me and decide if I'm worthy of your time and attention.  

If you'd rather check out my posts, [click here](posts).

If you wish to add my blog to your RSS feed, [here's the link](/index.xml).

### About me

<img src="https://hyperimages.net/photos/70/photo-0.jpeg" width="300">

This is how I look as of February 2026.

I live in Istanbul, Turkey.

I'm a developer by trade, sysadmin at heart. I love building things, but I really shine when everything's on fire.

I love books. They were always my greatest source of comfort. (This is probably why I want to write too.) I usually read non-fiction, either philosophy or politics. For fiction, I like detective stories and fantasy. My favorite book is [Unsong](https://unsongbook.com/) by Scott Alexander. I have a tattoo of Stephen King's Dark Tower on my back.

I love art. I'd rather be in a museum than at a party. I've been practicing sculpting since August 2025. My first *serious* sculpture, *[[./posts/syzygy photolog|Syzygy]]*, was exhibited in May 2026.

I hate politics. I mean I hate the spectacle where billionaires and their puppets pretend to represent us, not the power relations between agents in society. I'm a leftist. To be precise, I'm a Marxist. This means I'm convinced by Marx's critique of political economy and I'm a committed member of a socialist organization.

My other blogs:
- [ege.dev](https://ege.dev) for technical posts about programming, Linux, Kubernetes and databases.
- [bengidoom.com](https://bengidoom.com) for political posts about Turkey, in Turkish.
- [hyperimages.net](https://hyperimages.net) for photos of me and by me.

You think you don't have a story? I bet you do. But you don't need to tell anything. Just email me to say hi!
